Paul wrote these words while suffering constantly. He called it a "light affliction," not because it was easy, but because he set it next to an eternity with God.

Chronic illness can make you feel invisible or like a burden. You are neither. God's care for you doesn't depend on how productive you are.

On hard days, keep your prayers small. Ask for strength for the next hour. Let others help you; letting yourself be cared for is also an act of trust.
