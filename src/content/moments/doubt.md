A desperate father brought his sick son to Jesus and admitted he wasn't sure. His prayer was half faith, half doubt. Jesus didn't send him away. He healed his son.

Doubt is not the opposite of faith; it's often part of the journey. Thomas doubted, and Jesus showed him His hands.

Keep seeking. Read one of the Gospels slowly, ask your questions out loud, and talk with a pastor or a mature believer you trust. God promises to be found by those who search for Him with all their heart.
