Losing a job can shake your sense of who you are. But your identity was never your job title. You are a child of God, and He has not stopped caring for you.

Jesus told His followers not to worry about tomorrow, not because tomorrow doesn't matter, but because their Father already knows what they need (Matthew 6:32-34).

Pray, then take one practical step today: update your résumé, reach out to one person in your network, or ask your church if they know of openings. Faith and action go together.
