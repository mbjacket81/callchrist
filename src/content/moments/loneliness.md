Loneliness can whisper that you don't matter. Scripture says the opposite: the God who made you goes *with* you and will not fail you.

Jesus Himself knew loneliness. His closest friends fell asleep while He prayed in the garden, and they ran when He was arrested. He understands what you feel.

One small step can help. Send a text to one person today, even just "thinking of you." Or visit a church service or small group this week. God often answers loneliness through other people.
