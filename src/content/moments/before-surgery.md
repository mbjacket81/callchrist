Isaiah promises "perfect peace" to the person whose mind is stayed on God. You don't need to feel brave. You only need to turn your thoughts toward Him.

Psalm 91 is a favorite in hospital rooms. Read it slowly the night before, or have someone read it to you.

It can help to pray for each person who will care for you by role: the surgeon, the anesthesiologist, the nurses. You're not alone in that room.
