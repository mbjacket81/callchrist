Anxiety can make you feel like you have to solve everything before you can rest. Paul, who wrote "be careful for nothing" (don't be anxious about anything) from a prison cell, points us somewhere else: not to a solution, but to a Person.

Notice that he doesn't say the problem disappears. He says God's peace will *keep*, or stand guard over, your heart and mind. You can pray before you have answers.

If it helps, breathe slowly as you pray. Breathe in: *"You care for me."* Breathe out: *"I give this to You."* Repeat it as many times as you need.
