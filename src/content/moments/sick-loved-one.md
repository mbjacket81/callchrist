prayer: |
  Lord Jesus, You healed the sick and comforted the hurting. I bring someone I love to You right now.
  Strengthen them in their body and their spirit. Give wisdom to their doctors and nurses. Ease their pain and give them rest.
  And give me strength too. Help me love them well, trust You with what I can't control, and lean on Your grace when I feel weak.

When people brought their sick friends and family to Jesus, He welcomed them. You can do the same in prayer, as often as you need to.

It's okay to ask boldly for healing. It's also okay to admit you're tired and afraid. God's grace is enough for the person in the hospital bed and for the person sitting beside it.

Ask others to pray with you. If you'd like, write your loved one's name into the prayer above and pray it each morning.
