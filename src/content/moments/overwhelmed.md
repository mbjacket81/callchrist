David prayed Psalm 61 "from the end of the earth," as far from steady ground as he could feel. His prayer wasn't for everything to be fixed. It was to be led to a rock higher than himself.

When you're overwhelmed, you need something solid that isn't you. God is that rock.

Try writing down everything pressing on you. Pray over the list, then pick just one thing to do next. You can take the rest one step at a time.
