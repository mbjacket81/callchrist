Guilt can be a gift when it leads us back to God. Shame is different: it tells you that you *are* your mistake. Scripture answers that lie: in Christ there is *no condemnation*.

Confession isn't about earning forgiveness. It's agreeing with God about what happened and receiving what He already offers.

If your wrong hurt someone else, ask God for the courage to apologize or make it right. Then let it go. He has removed it as far as the east is from the west.
