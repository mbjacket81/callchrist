Grief isn't a sign that your faith is weak. Jesus knew He was about to raise Lazarus, and He still wept with the people who loved him (John 11:35). Your tears matter to Him.

Some days you may only be able to pray one sentence, or none at all. That's okay. Sitting in silence before God is prayer too.

Let someone walk with you through this: a friend, a pastor, or a grief group at a local church. God often sends His comfort through the people around us.
