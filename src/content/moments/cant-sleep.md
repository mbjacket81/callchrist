You don't have to stay awake to keep your world from falling apart. Psalm 121 reminds us that God never slumbers. He is keeping watch, so you can let go.

Try putting your phone away and praying Psalm 4:8 slowly, a few words with each breath. If worries keep coming, name each one and say, "Lord, this is Yours tonight."

If sleeplessness goes on for weeks, it's wise to talk with a doctor too. Caring for your body is part of caring for the life God gave you.
