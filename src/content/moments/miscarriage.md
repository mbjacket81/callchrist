Pregnancy loss is often a quiet grief. Others may not know what to say, or may not know at all. But your child was real, and your grief is real.

Psalm 56:8 paints a tender picture: God keeps your tears as if in a bottle. Not one is forgotten.

There is no "right" timeline for this kind of grief. Let yourself rest. Consider telling a trusted friend or your pastor, and ask your doctor about pregnancy loss support groups in your area.
