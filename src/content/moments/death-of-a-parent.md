Paul doesn't tell grieving believers not to sorrow. He tells them not to sorrow *as those who have no hope*. Grief and hope can live in the same heart.

Losing a parent can change how you see yourself, your family, and even your own life. Be patient with yourself as you find your footing.

Write down a few memories, stories, or sayings you want to keep. Share them with family. Remembering together is part of healing.
