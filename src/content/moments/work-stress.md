Paul wrote Colossians 3:23 to people with far less control over their work than most of us. His point: your work matters to God, even when no one else notices.

Galatians 6:9 is for the weary. Keep doing what's right; it isn't wasted, even when you can't see the results yet.

Burnout is real. Take your breaks, protect a day of rest, and talk with someone you trust if work is harming your health or family.
