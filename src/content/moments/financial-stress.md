Jesus doesn't dismiss our needs. He says your Father *knows* you need food and clothing. Your bills are not too small or too ordinary for Him.

Hebrews 13:5 ties contentment to a promise: God will never leave you. Your security rests on His presence, not your balance.

Pray, then take one practical step: list your bills, call a creditor about a payment plan, or ask your church about benevolence help or a budgeting class. Many churches offer both.
