Scripture doesn't say anger is always wrong. Jesus Himself was angry at injustice. But Paul warns: *be angry, and sin not.* The feeling isn't the problem; what we do with it can be.

James gives a simple order: hear first, speak second, get angry last.

If you can, step away for a few minutes before responding. Pray honestly, even bluntly. God would rather hear your anger than have you hide it.
