Paul's promise is practical: there is always *a way to escape*. Sometimes it's as simple as leaving the room, putting the phone down, or calling a friend. Look for the door, and take it.

Jesus is not disappointed in you for being tempted. He was tempted in every way we are, and He understands.

If you're fighting an addiction, you don't have to do it alone. Talk with a pastor, join a recovery group, or call the SAMHSA National Helpline at 1-800-662-4357 (free, confidential, 24/7).
