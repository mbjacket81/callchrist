David wrote Psalm 56 while he was running for his life. He didn't say "I'm never afraid." He said, "*What time* I am afraid, I will trust in thee." Fear came, and he answered it with trust.

You can do the same. Fear is a feeling; trust is a choice you can make while you still feel it.

Say Isaiah 41:10 out loud, slowly. Notice how many times God says "I will": strengthen, help, uphold. Those promises don't depend on how brave you feel.
