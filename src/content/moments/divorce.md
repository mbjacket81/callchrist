Divorce brings grief, anger, fear, and sometimes relief, often all in the same day. You can bring every one of those feelings to God.

Isaiah 54:10 is a promise for when the ground is shifting: even if mountains move, God's kindness toward you won't.

You'll face many decisions in this season. Lean on wise people: a pastor, a counselor, a trusted friend. If you are in danger at home, call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233 or 911.
