The writer of Psalm 42 talks to his own soul: *Why are you so downcast?* He doesn't hide his sadness from God. He brings it right into his prayer, and then he points himself back toward hope: *I shall yet praise him.*

Depression is not a lack of faith. Many faithful people in scripture, from Elijah to Jeremiah, went through seasons of deep darkness.

God often works through doctors, counselors, and friends. Asking for help is not giving up on God; it can be one of the ways He answers.
