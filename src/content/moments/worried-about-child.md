In the story of the prodigal son, the father saw his son "a great way off." He had been watching the road. Jesus told that story to show us how God waits for His children, and how parents can keep hoping.

You can't control your child's choices, especially as they grow older. You can pray, love, and keep the relationship open.

Ask a few trusted friends to pray with you by name. Parenting a struggling child is lonely; you don't have to carry it alone.
