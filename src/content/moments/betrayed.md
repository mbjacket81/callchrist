Judas shared meals with Jesus for years before betraying Him with a kiss. Jesus knows the particular pain of being hurt by someone close.

Romans 12:19 doesn't ask you to pretend the wrong didn't happen. It invites you to hand the job of justice to God, so it doesn't consume you.

Forgiveness is often a process, not a moment. You may need to pray this prayer many times. That's okay. Each time, you're choosing freedom.
