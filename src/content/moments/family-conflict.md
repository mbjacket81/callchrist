Paul's words are realistic: *if it be possible, as much as lieth in you.* You can't control how others respond. You can choose how you show up.

Forgiveness doesn't mean pretending nothing happened or giving up healthy boundaries. It means releasing the debt to God and refusing to let bitterness rule you.

Before the next hard conversation, pray for the person you're in conflict with by name. It's hard to stay hardened against someone you're praying for.
