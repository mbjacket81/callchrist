Abraham waited decades for a son. Joseph waited years in prison. David was anointed king long before he wore a crown. In scripture, waiting is often where God does His deepest work.

Waiting on God isn't sitting still. It's continuing to trust, pray, and do the next right thing while you don't yet see the answer.

Psalm 27:14 says "wait on the LORD" twice. Sometimes we need the reminder twice too. Be of good courage; He hasn't forgotten you.
