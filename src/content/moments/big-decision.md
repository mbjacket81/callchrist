James says God gives wisdom "liberally" and "upbraideth not": He won't scold you for asking. You can bring Him the same question as many times as you need.

Trusting God doesn't mean switching off your mind. Gather the facts, weigh the options, and ask advice from people who know you and love God. Then acknowledge Him in the choice.

It can help to write out each option and pray over them one at a time. Notice where you feel peace, and where you feel unrest.
