Many of the Psalms were written by people who felt abandoned by God. "How long wilt thou forget me, O LORD?" (Psalm 13:1). Feeling far from God is part of many believers' stories.

Psalm 139 is the other half of the truth: there is nowhere you can go that is outside His presence. Your feelings are real, but they don't measure how close He is.

Keep showing up. Read a Psalm a day, pray even when it feels dry, and gather with other believers. Distance often closes quietly, a step at a time.
