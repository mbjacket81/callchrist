The "threefold cord" in Ecclesiastes is a picture many couples hold onto: two people, bound together with God, are stronger than either alone.

Kindness, tenderness, and forgiveness rarely start with the other person. They start with the one who is praying. Ask God what your next step is, not your spouse's.

Consider a Christian marriage counselor or talking with your pastor. Seeking help is a sign of commitment, not failure. If you are ever unsafe, call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233.
