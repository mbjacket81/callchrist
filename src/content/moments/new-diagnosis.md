Moses spoke Deuteronomy 31:8 to Joshua before he faced a future he couldn't see. The promise is that God doesn't just walk with you; He goes *before* you, into every appointment and every result.

It's normal to feel numb, afraid, or overwhelmed after hard news. You don't have to have the right words. "Lord, help me" is a full prayer.

Write down your questions before your next appointment, and bring someone with you if you can. Let your church know, so they can pray for you and help.
